GLAMBYOYIN — video drop-in folder

Add these exact filenames here and the site picks them up automatically
(config lives in js/videos.js — nothing else needs to change):

  hero.mp4       — landscape, plays full-bleed behind the homepage wordmark
  look-01.mp4    — portrait, reveals itself while scrolling ("In Motion")
  look-02.mp4    — landscape, full-width with a gentle scale on scroll
  look-03.mp4    — portrait, the final full-bleed moment before the CTA

Guidelines:
  - H.264 MP4, muted-safe audio (videos autoplay muted; audio track is fine
    but will not be heard until a visitor unmutes, if controls are added)
  - Keep files reasonably compressed (a few MB each) for fast mobile loads
  - Each video also has a poster image in images/posters/ — swap those too
    if you want a real first-frame image instead of the placeholder art
